How the new format works
  • Super Series: the three lowest-ranked qualifiers play a round robin in Windhoek. The winner joins the group stage.
  • Group Stage: two groups of six. Top three in each group, plus the best fourth-placed team, go through.
  • Super 7: a full round robin of 21 matches. Slots are pre-seeded: A1 India, A2 Australia, A3 Pakistan, B1 New Zealand, B2 South Africa, B3 Sri Lanka, AB4 England. If a seeded team misses out, the team that finishes in that slot takes its fixtures.
  • Knockouts: 1st v 4th and 2nd v 3rd in the semi-finals, final at the Wanderers on 21 November.
